# Keys for Cure Domain Glossary

Domain language for the Keys for Cure digital concert management, attendee sign-in system, and multi-concept theme architecture.

## Language

**Concert Event**:
A published classical music charity performance with designated date, venue, musical program, and beneficiary organization.
_Avoid_: Show, gig, session

**RSVP Registration**:
A confirmed commitment by a patron to attend a concert, capturing guest count, attendee details, and optional charitable pledge.
_Avoid_: Sign-up, order, checkout

**Concert Pass**:
A digital verification credential issued to a registered attendee, carrying a unique booking reference and cryptographic QR code.
_Avoid_: Ticket, receipt, voucher

**Organizer Door Portal**:
A secured mobile-optimized scanner interface used by venue volunteers to validate passes and record attendance at the door.
_Avoid_: Admin backend, scanner app

**Door Check-In**:
The atomic physical act of verifying an attendee's Concert Pass at the venue entrance and recording their arrival in real time.
_Avoid_: Sign-in, admission, entry scan

**Theme Concept**:
One of three distinctly styled front-end layout presentations mirroring established global musical charities, switchable dynamically with zero loss of content or backend functionality.
_Avoid_: Skin, template, child theme

**Benchmark Charity**:
An internationally established musical nonprofit whose visual language, typography, and section hierarchy serve as the design archetype for a Theme Concept.
_Avoid_: Competitor, clone site

**Exemplar Content**:
Authentic organizational history, leadership credentials (Nick Wang & Young Musicians), government ministerial endorsements (Christopher Luxon, Hon Paul Goldsmith), and hospital partner disclosures (Starship Children's Hospital) sourced directly from `keysforcure.org.nz`.
_Avoid_: Placeholder text, lorem ipsum, dummy data
